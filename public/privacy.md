# Privacy and cookies, BR4M

> Optional Google tools stay off until you choose. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.

Canonical page: https://br4m.tv/privacy

BR4M is the controller for br4m.tv. Contact: Discord (https://discord.gg/Unb7Y2GxNT).

Optional cookies are Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-DJJBGLYNVJ) and YouTube. Both are Google services. They do not run before consent. Necessary storage remembers this choice, and a currency if you pick one.

## Do any optional cookies run before I choose?

No. Google Analytics and YouTube stay off until you accept them, together or one by one.

## Can I reject Analytics but still play films?

Yes. Customize, turn on YouTube only, and save. Or allow YouTube when the player asks. Analytics stays off.

## How do I change my mind?

Use Cookie settings on the privacy page, or Cookies in the footer. Rejecting later stops Analytics and new YouTube embeds on br4m.tv.

Films play on youtube.com so a play can count as a YouTube view. Shop, membership and donations are hosted by Fourthwall.
