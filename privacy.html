Privacy

Optional Google tools stay off until you choose. Rejecting is as easy as accepting.

Who is responsible

BR4M

BR4M is the controller for this website (br4m.tv). Contact for privacy questions is Discord. Open Discord.

Your choice

Cookies

This site uses strictly necessary storage and, only if you allow them, two Google services: Google Analytics 4 and YouTube. Nothing optional runs before you pick. You can accept all, reject optional, or set each one. You can change that later.

Google Analytics

If you allow Analytics, the site loads Google tag G-DJJBGLYNVJ. That tag can see which pages you open, roughly where you are (not a precise address), your device type, and referral. IP addresses are truncated. Google Signals and ads personalisation stay off. Legal basis: consent.

Google LLC is in the United States. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google's privacy policy and Google's cookie page.

YouTube

Films and trailers play in the BR4M player on youtube.com, not youtube-nocookie, so a play can count as a YouTube view. That loads Google's player and YouTube cookies. Like and Comment open YouTube in a new tab. Legal basis: consent. If you refuse, you can still open the film on YouTube itself.

A muted background reel on the movies hub also uses YouTube. It only starts after YouTube is allowed.

Other sites

Shop, membership and donations are hosted by Fourthwall. When you leave for those pages, Fourthwall's terms and privacy policy apply. Discord, TikTok and YouTube as destinations are their own services too.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or to object. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier lawful use. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In California, we do not sell personal information, and a Global Privacy Control signal is treated as an opt-out of Analytics.

This site is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from them through Analytics or YouTube embeds without a parent or guardian making the cookie choice.

How long

Your cookie choice is stored on this device for 13 months, then we ask again. Analytics data follows the Google Analytics property settings. YouTube cookies last as long as Google sets them. We cannot delete cookies that live on youtube.com. Turning YouTube off here stops new embeds from this site.

Quick facts

Do any optional cookies run before I choose?
No. Google Analytics and YouTube stay off until you accept them, together or one by one.
Can I reject Analytics but still play films?
Yes. Customize, turn on YouTube only, and save. Or allow YouTube when the player asks. Analytics stays off.
How do I change my mind?
Use Cookie settings on this page, or Cookies in the footer. Rejecting later stops Analytics and new YouTube embeds on br4m.tv.
