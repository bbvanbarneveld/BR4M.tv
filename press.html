Quick facts

Who is BR4M?
BR4M is a filmmaker who writes, builds and films original series inside Minecraft. The official site is br4m.tv.
What is The First Word?
The First Word is the first film in The Architects, a scripted trilogy. It premieres 30.08.2026 at 15:00 CEST on YouTube and can be played on br4m.tv/movies.
What is The Architects?
The Architects is a scripted Minecraft trilogy. Three command blocks. Three hidden locations. Whoever holds all three can rewrite the world.
How do I watch BR4M films?
Play them on br4m.tv/movies in the BR4M player, or on YouTube at @br4mtv. Like and comments live on YouTube.
What is BR4M+?
BR4M+ is the membership on Fourthwall. Members watch ad free on the members site and get more films, extras and member perks. Tiers and prices live on the shop, not on this site.
How do I contact BR4M for press?
Reach BR4M on Discord. Stills and type are on this page.
