# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

English-speaking Minecraft viewers, film fans, press, and supporters who land here to watch BR4M's films, wait for premieres, buy merch, or join BR4M+. They arrive from YouTube, Discord, TikTok, or a shared link, often on a phone, and they want the films, not a generic creator homepage.

## Product Purpose

BR4M.tv is the cinematic brand surface for BR4M, a filmmaker who writes, builds and films original series inside Minecraft. It sells the films and the world around them: movies, the next premiere, the shop, membership, and support. Success is that a first-time visitor feels they found a film studio, not a YouTube channel with a website, and that they can watch, calendar, shop, or support without leaving the brand.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, expensive, quiet. Imagery and framing do the talking. Type is big and calm. Violet is the only accent, used sparingly. Copy is English, plain, and never theatrical. Three words: cinematic, expensive, quiet.

## Anti-references

- Faux-TV jargon and broadcast costume: "Transmission 001", "On air", "Signal", channel numbers
- Space / starfield styling
- Generic card-grid creator landings
- SaaS metric heroes, identical icon cards, and tracked eyebrows on every section
- Listing BR4M+ perks, tiers, or prices that live on Fourthwall
- Framing the whole site around a single series

## Design Principles

- Cinema is expressed through stills, letterbox, grain, and scale, never through TV-channel copy
- The site is about BR4M the filmmaker; THE ARCHITECTS is one series among more to come
- Show the films, do not decorate around them
- Every interactive element answers on hover; reduced motion is a first-class path, not an afterthought
- Honest empty states over invented catalog, shipping, or perk claims

## Accessibility & Inclusion

English-only visitor-facing copy. Skip link on every page. `prefers-reduced-motion` disables the custom cursor, scroll reveals, and autorotation, and opens the letterbox instantly. Touch devices hide the custom cursor. Body contrast should stay at or above WCAG AA on the black surface; muted type must not drop below readable white alphas. No time-limited traps: premiere buttons stay disabled until the real URL exists rather than counting down into a dead click.
